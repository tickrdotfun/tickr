## A minimal indexer

Five log streams rebuild everything the site shows, with no tickr service in the loop:

1. **Launches.** `TokenLaunched` on the factory, from its start block in [07 addresses](./07-addresses.md). It names the coin, its pool id, the deployer and the pair. A coin launched through a launcher fires it too; the launcher's own `Launched` and `FirstBuy` land in the same transaction and say which ticker it sits under and what the opening buy took.
2. **Trades.** `Swap` on the pool manager, filtered by pool id. Amounts are from the pool's side: the currency it took in is negative and includes the fee, the one it paid out is positive, so a negative delta on the coin's side is a sell and a positive one a buy. Only the pool manager holds reserves, so there is no internal swap to skip. A `SnipeTaxed` on the coin in the same transaction says how much of a buy in the first five seconds went to the dead address.
3. **Fees.** `FeesCollected` on the locker, one per collection, with the split already applied: the protocol's, the creator's and the club's parts of the quote side, and what the coin side credited and burned. The sum of `burnedCoin` is the dead address balance.
4. **The club.** `ClubFee`, `ClubClaimed` and `DeadPotSwept` on the ticker launcher, keyed by ticker and epoch.
5. **The buyback.** `BoughtAndBurned` on the treasury, one per buy of TICKR.

Two rules of thumb. Backfill in bounded block ranges: the public RPC refuses a wide `eth_getLogs` window. And index a launch before the trades of the same block: the opening buy lands in the launch transaction.

## Holder counts

A coin's `Transfer` log is its holder ledger. Leave these addresses out when counting holders; they hold coins for the system, not for themselves:

- the pool manager: the pool's reserves;
- the launch locker and the fee escrow: fees in transit and creator credits waiting to be claimed;
- `0x000000000000000000000000000000000000dEaD`: burned;
- the launch seeder, the zap router, the launch-and-buy router and the ticker launcher: pass-through only, holding nothing between transactions.

| `SymbolTooLong`, `BadSymbol` | ticker launcher | an invented name is over twelve bytes, or has a character that is not a letter or a digit. |
| `NotAMint` | locker | somebody tried to transfer an existing position into the locker; only launch mints land there. |
| `OnlyTickerLauncher` | seeder | only the ticker launcher named on the factory may open a dollar pool. |
| `OnlyExecutor`, `NotOneDollar`, `OutsideTheBand`, `PriceGuard` | guard hook | the dollar pool refuses anything but the seeder's own opening, a position off the band, or a swap that would move the price. |

## Versioning and attribution

The contracts are immutable; a new version is a new factory and a new deployment record. If you build on tickr, say so and link the site; do not present your service as run by tickr.
